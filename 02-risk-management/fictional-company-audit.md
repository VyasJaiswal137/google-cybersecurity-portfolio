Botium Toys: Security Audit & Risk Assessment Report
Executive Summary
Botium Toys operates a storefront and an online retail presence supported by an adjoining warehouse and internal IT infrastructure. An internal security audit was conducted to evaluate the organization's existing security controls, assess compliance adherence against regulatory standards (PCI DSS, GDPR, SOC 1/SOC 2), and identify gaps in overall security posture.

The audit revealed an overall Risk Score of 8/10 (High Risk) due to a lack of critical technical controls, insufficient user access restrictions, missing disaster recovery capabilities, and non-compliance with major regulatory frameworks protecting customer data.

Audit Scope & Goals
Scope: Entire security program at Botium Toys, including physical on-premises assets, employee end-user devices, internal network, data storage/retention systems, legacy systems, and e-commerce/database software.

Goals: Evaluate existing assets, complete the controls and compliance assessment checklists, identify key vulnerabilities, and provide actionable remediation recommendations to reduce risk and ensure legal/regulatory compliance.
