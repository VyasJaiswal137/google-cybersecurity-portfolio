# Course 2 - Module 4: Incident Response Playbooks

## Playbook Overview & Structure

* **Definition:** A playbook is an operational manual providing a predefined, up-to-date list of steps to follow when identifying and responding to security incidents and vulnerabilities.
* **Core Components:**
  * **Strategy:** Outlines expectations for team members assigned to specific tasks and specifies the individuals responsible for execution.
  * **Plan:** Dictates the exact operational and technical procedures required to complete each task.

---

## Playbook Lifecycle & Updates

Playbooks are managed collaboratively as **living documents** that are continuously refined. Primary triggers for updating playbooks include:

* **Operational Failures:** Identifying oversights, gaps, or failures in existing security policies, procedures, or playbook steps.
* **Regulatory & Compliance Changes:** Adjustments in government-imposed laws, compliance requirements, or regional incident notification mandates (which vary by geographic location and affected data types).
* **Evolving Threat Landscape:** Adaptation to changing threat actor tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs).

---

## Incident & Vulnerability Response Playbooks

* **Alignment with Business Continuity:** Developed to support the organization's **Business Continuity Plan**—the established strategy that enables an organization to maintain or quickly recover normal operations during and after a disruption.
* **Specific Incident Vectors:** Tailored to address distinct threat types, such as Ransomware, Vishing, and Business Email Compromise (BEC).
* **Key Operational Benefits:**
  * **Standardization & Error Reduction:** Establishes consistency, minimizes human error, and enforces response timeframes.
  * **Legal & Regulatory Compliance:** Ensures actions adhere strictly to organizational protocols and external legal standards.
  * **Forensic Data Integrity:** Protects digital evidence from accidental mishandling or corruption during investigations.
  * **Urgent Risk Mitigation:** Enables rapid containment based on the core risk calculation: **Risk = Likelihood of a Threat**.

---

## Core Incident Response Lifecycle Steps

Incident and vulnerability playbooks structure the response process into sequential steps:

1. **Preparation:** Setting up tools, policies, playbooks, and security controls before an incident occurs.
2. **Detection:** Identifying suspicious activity, alerts, or system anomalies.
3. **Analysis:** Investigating threat data to confirm the incident, assess severity, and determine the root cause.
4. **Containment:** Isolating impacted systems or networks to stop the threat from spreading.
5. **Eradication:** Removing the threat from the environment (e.g., deleting malware, revoking compromised credentials).
6. **Recovery:** Restoring systems, applications, and operations to normal, secure functionality.
7. **Post-Incident Activities & Coordination:** Conducting lessons learned, updating playbooks, and coordinating communication across internal teams and external stakeholders.
