## Key Technical & Strategic Takeaways

### 1. Risk Management & Security Frameworks
* **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF):** Core framework built around five essential functions: *Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond,* and *Recover*.
* **NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF):** Seven-step process (*Prepare, Categorize, Select, Implement, Assess, Authorize, Monitor*) used to manage risk across organizational life cycles.
* **OWASP Principles:** Fundamental application security guidelines focusing on defense in depth, failing securely, establishing secure defaults, and minimizing attack surfaces.

### 2. Security Control Classifications
* **Administrative Controls:** Policy-driven safeguards focused on workforce behavior, including *Least Privilege*, *Separation of Duties*, *Disaster Recovery Plans (DRP)*, and password management policies.
* **Technical Controls:** Technology-based solutions to safeguard assets, including *Firewalls*, *Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)*, *Antivirus (AV)*, and end-to-end data *Encryption*.
* **Physical Controls:** Tangible security mechanisms that protect facility access, including *Physical Locks*, *CCTV Surveillance*, *Badge Readers*, and *Fire Suppression Systems*.
* **Control Functional Types:** Categorizing security mechanisms based on operational behavior:
  * **Preventative:** Stops security incidents before they occur (e.g., Firewalls, RBAC).
  * **Detective:** Identifies active or ongoing security incidents (e.g., IDS, CCTV).
  * **Corrective:** Restores systems and assets after an incident occurs (e.g., Backups, DRP).
  * **Deterrent:** Discourages malicious activity (e.g., Warning signs, Encryption).

### 3. Regulatory Compliance & Data Protection Standards
* **PCI DSS:** Safeguards cardholder data by mandating secure environments, access restrictions, robust password rules, and encryption across transaction pipelines.
* **GDPR:** Strict European privacy regulation requiring protection of PII/SPII, transparent data collection policies, and mandatory 72-hour breach notifications.
* **SOC 1 & SOC 2:** Trust services criteria evaluating controls related to data confidentiality, availability, processing integrity, and privacy.

### 4. SIEM Tools & Incident Response Playbooks
* **SIEM Operations:** Centralized platforms (such as Chronicle and Splunk) that aggregate, analyze, and visualize security log data from across an enterprise network.
* **Incident Response Playbooks:** Standardized, step-by-step procedure guides enabling security analysts to triage alerts, contain threats, and execute consistent incident response actions.
