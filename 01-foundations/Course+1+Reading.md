## 📜 Frameworks, Regulations & Compliance

| Term | Full Name / Description | Core Focus & Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AICPA** | American Institute of Certified Public Accountants | Created SOC 1 and SOC 2 auditing standards for risk assessment[cite: 1]. |
| **CIP** | Critical Infrastructure Protection | Reliability standards enforced on energy and electric power grid organizations[cite: 1]. |
| **CIS** | Center for Internet Security | Nonprofit providing actionable security controls to defend systems against attacks[cite: 1]. |
| **FedRAMP** | Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program | US government program standardizing security authorization for cloud services[cite: 1]. |
| **FERC-NERC** | Federal Energy Regulatory Commission - NERC | Regulatory body enforcing cybersecurity compliance on power grid infrastructure[cite: 1]. |
| **GDPR** | General Data Protection Regulation | EU law protecting residents' data privacy and requiring breach notification within 72 hours[cite: 1]. |
| **HIPAA** | Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act | US federal law safeguarding confidential patient health information from unauthorized sharing[cite: 1]. |
| **HITRUST** | Health Information Trust Alliance | Security framework assisting health organizations in achieving HIPAA compliance[cite: 1]. |
| **ICJ** | International Court of Justice | International judicial body setting strict legal parameters for lawful cyber counterattacks[cite: 1]. |
| **ISO** | International Organization for Standardization | Establishes global cross-border standards for technology and operational management[cite: 1]. |
| **NIST** | National Institute of Standards and Technology | US agency developing voluntary, industry-standard cybersecurity frameworks[cite: 1]. |
| **NIST CSF** | NIST Cybersecurity Framework | Guidelines designed to help organizations manage and mitigate cyber risks[cite: 1]. |
| **NIST RMF** | NIST Risk Management Framework | Process for integrating security and risk management into system lifecycles[cite: 1]. |
| **PCI DSS** | Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard | International security standard aimed at securing credit card handling and reducing fraud[cite: 1]. |
| **SOC Reports** | System and Organization Controls (Type 1 & 2) | Audit reports evaluating user access policies, financial compliance, and data safety[cite: 1]. |

## ⚠️ Threats, Attacks & Data Types

| Term | Full Name / Description | Core Focus & Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **APT** | Advanced Persistent Threat | Highly skilled threat actors seeking stealthy, long-term unauthorized network access[cite: 1]. |
| **BEC** | Business Email Compromise | Phishing attack impersonating known sources to trick victims for financial gain[cite: 1]. |
| **PHI** | Protected Health Information | Medical data related to health status, care provision, or healthcare payment[cite: 1]. |
| **PII** | Personally Identifiable Information | Standard data used to infer an individual's identity (e.g., full name, phone number)[cite: 1]. |
| **SPII** | Sensitive Personally Identifiable Information | High-risk personal details requiring strict handling (e.g., Social Security numbers)[cite: 1]. |
| **USB** | Universal Serial Bus | Physical hardware interface commonly exploited in USB baiting attacks[cite: 1]. |
