# Module 1: Common Cybersecurity Terminology

> 💡 **Core Concepts & Definitions**

---

### 📋 Rules, Frameworks & Posture

* **Compliance:** Following internal company policies and external government laws to avoid heavy fines and security breaches.
* **Security Frameworks:** Step-by-step guidelines used to build security plans that protect data and privacy.
* **Security Controls:** Specific safety measures (safeguards) designed to lower targeted security risks.
* **Security Posture:** An organization's overall ability to defend its data and react when threats change. *(A stronger posture means less risk for the company.)*

---

### 🎯 Threat Types

* **Threat Actor:** Any person or group that creates a security risk to computers, applications, networks, or data.
* **Internal Threat:** A current or former employee, vendor, or trusted partner who poses a risk to the company.

  * **Accidental:** Someone making a mistake, like accidentally clicking a bad link in an email.
  * **Intentional:** Someone knowingly doing risky or harmful things, like stealing private data.

---

### 🌐 Protection Areas & Tasks

* **Network Security:** Protecting an organization's network, devices, systems, and data from unauthorized users.
* **Cloud Security:** Securing data, apps, and servers stored in remote data centers accessed over the internet.
* **Programming:** Writing clear step-by-step instructions for a computer to run tasks automatically. Security analysts use it to:

  * Automate repetitive tasks (such as checking lists of dangerous websites).
  * Review web traffic for issues.
  * Send instant alerts when suspicious activity happens.
